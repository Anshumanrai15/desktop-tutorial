Backtests and paper trading are hypothetical

  • Past performance does not guarantee future results. A strategy that worked historically may lose money in the future.
  • Backtests are simulations. Real trading involves costs, taxes, slippage, liquidity limits, gaps, order rejections and emotions that a simulation cannot fully capture.
  • The stock list is today's Nifty 100, so long backtests include survivorship bias: companies that fell out of the index are not included, which can make results look better than they would have been.
  • Paper trading uses simulated money only. No real orders are placed with any exchange or broker.
