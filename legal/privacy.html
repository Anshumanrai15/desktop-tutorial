Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: there are no accounts and we don't collect your personal data. Your settings and paper-trading portfolio are saved only in your own browser, on your own device.

What stays on your device

The app saves the following in your browser's local storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) so it can remember them between visits:

  • your paper-trading account, holdings and order history
  • your recent searches, chosen stock, strategy, indicators and backtest settings
  • any CSV files you import
  • whether you have dismissed the first-visit notice

This information is not sent to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. This also resets your paper portfolio.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number or any login. The site currently uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy before it happens.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Donations and referral links

  • UPI donations are processed entirely by your UPI app and bank. We only receive what UPI normally shows a payee, such as the payer's name or UPI ID and the amount. We use this only to keep records of donations received, as the law requires.
  • Broker referral links take you to the broker's own website. Anything you enter there is governed by that broker's privacy policy. The broker may tell us that an account was opened through our link, but not your personal details beyond what their referral programme shares.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 18.

Your rights

Because we do not store your personal data, there is usually nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold any personal data about you (for example, from a donation), contact us and we will help, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
